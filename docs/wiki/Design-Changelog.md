# Design changelog

_Last updated 2026-11-14. Source: the [design canvas](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ab065fd4-9113-4368-b970-3f623da00a00)._

Newest first.

## 2026-11-13

- Added the World, a pixel-art map you explore in a dream, set inside a body: eight lands sit where their part of the body is (eyes, brain, immune system, heart, bones, muscles, skin and blood), each with a hazard tied to the job its status hits. It has an atlas on the web and Mac, a world map, walking and field notes on iPhone, and an iPad view.
- Replaced the battle screen with the Scope: you are a scientist in the Professor's lab, and an encounter is a slide under his microscope showing what a land's hazard is doing.
- Added the Lab and the Kitchen as places where the story happens, with chapters, the Professor's lines, and cooking along with a recipe in the Kitchen. The Lab on the web and Mac gained a Tonight card.
- Added evolutions: every food grows from where it starts (a sprout, blossom, sapling or nest) to the food itself to an heirloom, and its abilities are the vitamins and minerals it is a real source of. The sprouts were then redrawn so each one sits in its soil.

- Added account screens: sign-in, settings, profile, your data and privacy, friends and a friend's card on iPhone, and settings in the Lab. The trainer card's Skins button became a gear that opens Settings, where skins live.
- Added the Plus tier: plans and pricing sheet, paywall, Ask the professor, recipe ideas and the weekly letter. Suggested price $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year.
- Added the Health section on every platform: label and barcode scanning, logging with portions and meals, the health score, nutrient detail, the iPad view, the Lab report and scan review. Health replaces Map in the iPhone tab bar.
- Added iPad versions (Dex with entry panel, party and cook, regions) and the web and Mac Lab (home and research desk), with build notes on every game screen.
- Redesigned the whole UX as a game: research tiers, seen and caught, regions, moves, abilities and forms, parties as recipes, a trainer card and badges, in a full game UI.
- The carrot entry screen was made taller so its forms and cautions are no longer cut off.

## 2026-11-12

- Journey map of every screen, the professor (a chef rat guide), and three directions: Living wall, Field journal and Night market.
- Field journal: replaced the latest plate with a recipe carousel, and added the interactive recipe screen with the spanning-cell grid and the pot animation.
